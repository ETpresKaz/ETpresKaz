ABOUT ME

I'm a Computer Science student at Purdue University, focused on building software products and exploring intellegent systems.

My work spans full-stack application development, robotics, artificial intelligence, and physics simulation.

I enjoy trying new things, and then taking those projects from an initial concept to a function system.

Currently focused on:

* Checkpoint — Building a social accountability platform.
* FriendHand — Developing a robotic arm with kinematics and computer vision.
* Orbital Simulator — Simulating spacecraft motion and orbital mechanics.
* NASA SUITS — Developing voice-enabled AI systems for astronaut assistance.
